  Q1. Is it expensive to study in China?
      No. China is one of the most affordable study destinations. Tuition is low compared to Western countries, and many students study on full or partial scholarships such as CSC, which can cover tuition, accommodation and a monthly stipend.

  Q2. Do I need to know Chinese to study in China?
      Not always. Thousands of programs are taught fully in English, especially at bachelor, master and PhD level. For Chinese-taught programs, an HSK certificate is required — and we help you prepare.

  Q3. What scholarships can Gujjify Consultant help me apply for?
      We assist with the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC), Confucius Institute Scholarship, Provincial scholarships and individual University scholarships, depending on your profile and program.

  Q4. How long does the admission process take?
      It varies by university and intake, but typically the admission process takes 4–10 weeks. Scholarship rounds follow fixed deadlines, so applying early is very important.

  Q5. Which documents do I need to apply?
      Generally: passport, academic transcripts and degrees, a study plan/personal statement, recommendation letters, a physical examination form, and a passport-size photo. We give you a personalised checklist.

  Q6. Is studying in China safe for international students?
      Yes. China is considered very safe, with modern campuses, good public transport and large, welcoming international student communities in most major cities.

  Q7. Do you help with the student visa?
      Yes. After your admission and JW202 form, we prepare your complete X1/X2 student visa application and guide you through the entire process.

  Q8. How do I get started?
