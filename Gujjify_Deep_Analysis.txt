SECTION 9 - RISKS, GAPS & DATA-QUALITY ISSUES
==========================================================================================

  1. Outdated offers - all data is 2024-2025; deadlines passed.
     -> website shows "typical month" + freshness disclaimer; client should refresh brochures
        for the 2027 intake.                                                        [CHECK]
  2. Agency branding in source brochures (MalishaEdu, IFFI; IFFI contacts pre-filled in the
     application form) - never reuse those documents or prices publicly.            [CHECK]
  3. University names removed - the finder must work without them (it does: city, province,
     ranking, 211/985 status, majors, funding).
  4. MBBS recognition - brochure says "BMDC & WHO listed" (Bangladesh-oriented); Pakistani
     students need PM&DC rules - never claim PM&DC recognition per programme.        [CHECK]
  5. Unverifiable marketing stats on the old site (1200+, 80+, 500+, 7+ years) - hidden until
     the client confirms; data-backed stats used instead.                            [CHECK]
  6. City typo in source: "Zhanjiang (Jiangsu)" -> treated as Zhenjiang, Jiangsu.     [CHECK]
  7. Language-course flyer promotes "stay legal in China" to job seekers - a student permit
     does not allow work; avoid that framing on the website.                         [CHECK]
  8. Living-cost and accommodation figures are benchmarks, not brochure data - labelled as
     editable estimates in the calculator.
  9. Keyword data has no volumes - the tool ranking is by breadth of demand (number of
     distinct queries and markets), which is appropriate for choosing features.

SECTION 10 - STRATEGY: THE WEBSITE BLUEPRINT
==========================================================================================

  10.1 POSITIONING
       "Your Gateway to Study in China" - a UK-registered consultancy with a Lahore office that
       shows its real programme map, real costs and honest eligibility before the first chat.
       Premium, calm, trustworthy (navy & gold), WhatsApp-first.

  10.2 AUDIENCES
       a) Pakistani students & parents (Matric/FSc, MBBS, engineering/CS, budget-sensitive,
          Roman-Urdu speakers) - primary.
       b) Global South students (South & Southeast Asia, Africa, Central Asia) searching in
          English and local languages - growth.
       c) Master/PhD candidates looking for fully funded offers (23 in data).

  10.3 HERO - 3D CHINA PROGRAMME MAP
       All 34 regions extruded; the 21 programme provinces raised in gold by programme count
       (Jiangsu tallest), 30 glowing city beacons, auto-tour by count, hover/tap info card
       (levels, cities, max stipend, sample majors) -> province page / finder / WhatsApp.
       SVG 2D fallback for no-WebGL / reduced motion; list view for accessibility.

  10.4 THE 3 GLOBAL TOOLS (Section 7.2 evidence)
       1. University & Programme Finder - filters by level, 9 study fields, major, province/city,
          teaching language, funding, no-IELTS, tuition, ranking; shortlist, compare, PDF list.
       2. Study Cost Calculator - by programme or level+city, scholarship scenario, dorm,
          lifestyle; brochure fees + living benchmarks; 42 currencies with live rates.
       3. Scholarship Eligibility Checker - 7-step quiz (incl. CGPA conversion, IELTS/Duolingo/
          HSK, bank statement) matched against every programme's rules; "unlock more" tips.
       Tools feed each other and all end in a pre-filled WhatsApp message.

  10.5 PAGES
       Home · Finder · Programme (72) · Province (21) · Tools hub · Cost · Eligibility · Services
       · About · Blog (9 seeds, to expand) · FAQ (8 + 5 drafts from real student questions) ·
       Contact · Privacy · Terms · Cookies · 404.

  10.6 SEO & CONTENT PLAN
       - 115-URL sitemap (static pages + 72 programme + 21 province + 9 blog pages).
       - Province pages target "study in <province/city>" and "living cost in <city>".
       - Blog priorities from keyword demand: CSC requirements 2026/27, China scholarship
         without IELTS, MBBS in China for Pakistani students (PM&DC), China student visa from
         Pakistan (X1), cost of living by city, CGPA to percentage for CSC, HSK levels explained,
         study plan / recommendation-letter templates, JW202 explained.
       - 301 redirects from the old PHP URLs (in public/.htaccess).

  10.7 CONVERSION
       Every tool result -> WhatsApp with a summary; floating WhatsApp button; shortlist share;
       "FREE scholarship eligibility assessment" offer (from the Instagram campaign).
